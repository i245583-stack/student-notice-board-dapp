Decentralised Student Notice Board

A simple dApp that stores and retrieves a student notice on the Sepolia test network.

How to use this dApp

  1. Connect your MetaMask wallet.
  2. Enter a notice and save it to the blockchain.
  3. Read the saved notice or clear it.
